One out-of-office auto-reply, as if a houseplant had written it — 100 characters.

> Out of office. The cat ate 3 of my leaves this week, so your email goes to him now. He types faster.

Run `python3 main.py`. It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.

The joke: no fern, no March, no spite — the cat ate three leaves this week, so the plant forwards your email to the cat. He types faster.
